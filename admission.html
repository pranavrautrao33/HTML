Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if I am eligible?
Review the eligibility information for your selected program and match your prior academic record with the minimum requirements.
What documents are required?
Generally you need an academic transcript, identity proof, a passport photo, and any supporting letters or certificates.
When is the application deadline?
Deadlines vary by program and intake. Check the admissions calendar for the current semester and apply early.
Can I change my program after submitting?
Yes, you can request a program change before the review process completes. Contact admissions support for help.
Do you offer financial aid?
Financial assistance and scholarships are available for eligible applicants. Submit your financial aid application alongside your admission form.
How will I receive my admission letter?
Admission decisions and offers are sent by email and through the student portal once the review is complete.
